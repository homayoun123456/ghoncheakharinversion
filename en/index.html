Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantity for dried rose buds is 100 kg. For essential oil, the minimum is 1 liter. We can also provide samples before placing bulk orders.

What certificates do you provide?

We provide Certificate of Analysis (COA), MRL test reports, Phytosanitary certificate, and organic certification upon request. All documents meet international export standards.

What are the payment terms?

We accept various payment methods including T/T (bank transfer), L/C (Letter of Credit), and Western Union. Payment terms can be negotiated based on order volume and relationship.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping time varies by destination: 15-20 days for Asia, 20-30 days for Europe, and 25-35 days for America. We offer both sea and air freight options.

Can you provide samples?

Yes, we provide samples for quality assessment. Sample costs vary based on product type and quantity. Shipping costs are typically covered by the buyer.
