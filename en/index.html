Why Lalehzar Rose?

Understanding the unique qualities of Iranian rose products from our premium region

01

Exceptional Growing Altitude (2700-3000m)

Lalehzar is one of the highest rose cultivation areas in the world, resulting in:

  • Unmatched aroma concentration
  • Natural pest reduction (near-zero MRL)
  • Higher active compound density (flavonoids & phenols)
02

Mountain Climate Benefits

Significant day-night temperature variation increases aromatic and medicinal compounds:

  • Higher volatile oil content
  • Stable Geraniol and Nerol
  • Longer-lasting fragrance after processing
03

Larger, Heavier Buds (Mainly M/L Size)

Lalehzar buds are superior due to:

  • Higher dry weight
  • Thicker petals
  • Optimal for industrial aroma extraction
  • Higher Citronellol content (key component)
04

Signature Aroma Profile

Scientifically richer fragrance due to:

  • High Citronellol percentage
  • Concentrated Geraniol
  • Phenyl Ethyl Alcohol (core flower scent)
  • Excellent stability after packaging
